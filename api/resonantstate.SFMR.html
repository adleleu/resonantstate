resonantstate.SFMR.ell2SFMR(p, e1, e2, vp1, vp2, m1, m2, P1, P2, lbd1, lbd2, return_all=False)[source]

Converts from elliptic elements to the coordinates \(\left(X, Y, X_2, Y_2, \delta\right)\) of the Second Fondamental Model of resonance (SFM). \(\left(X, Y\right)\) corresponds to the unique degree of freedom of the SFM and \(\left(X_2, Y_2\right)\) to the first integral. \(\delta\) is the unique parameter of the SFM The SFM is described byt the Hamiltonian \(\mathcal{H}(\Sigma,\sigma)=3(\delta+1)\Sigma-\Sigma^2+2\sqrt{2\Sigma}\cos\sigma\) where \(X+iY=\sqrt{2\Sigma}e^{i\sigma}\).

Author : Jeremy Couturier. https://jeremycouturier.com

Update 02/10/2026 By A. Leleu to refect the changes in the paper (removed the normalisation by Gamma, and chose Deltagamma=0, rename the variables)

Parameters:

  • p (int) – Integer such that the two planets are in resonance \(p\) : \(p+1\).

  • e1 (float or 1-dimensional numpy array of floats) – The eccentricity \(e_1\) of the inner planet.

  • e2 (float or 1-dimensional numpy array of floats) – The eccentricity \(e_2\) of the outer planet.

  • vp1 (float or 1-dimensional numpy array of floats) – The longitude of periapsis \(\varpi_1\) of the inner planet, in radians.

  • vp2 (float or 1-dimensional numpy array of floats) – The longitude of periapsis \(\varpi_2\) of the outer planet, in radians.

  • m1 (float or 1-dimensional numpy array of floats) – The mass \(m_1\) of the inner planet in units of the stellar mass.

  • m2 (float or 1-dimensional numpy array of floats) – The mass \(m_2\) of the outer planet in units of the stellar mass.

  • P1 (float or 1-dimensional numpy array of floats) – The period \(2\pi/n_1\) of the inner planet in any units. Such that \(\mathcal{G}\left(m_0+m_1\right)=n_1^2a_1^3\).

  • P2 (float or 1-dimensional numpy array of floats) – The period \(2\pi/n_2\) of the outer planet in the same units as P1. Such that \(\mathcal{G}\left(m_0+m_2\right)=n_2^2a_2^3\).

  • lbd1 (float or 1-dimensional numpy array of floats) – The mean longitude \(\lambda_1\) of the inner planet, in radians.

  • lbd2 (float or 1-dimensional numpy array of floats) – The mean longitude \(\lambda_2\) of the outer planet, in radians.

  • return_all (bool) – if return_all==False, the function returns \(\left[X, Y, X_2, Y_2, \delta\right]\), if return_all==True it return a dictionary with additional variables instead.

Returns:

l – The list \(\left[X, Y, X_2, Y_2, \delta\right]\) of elements of the Second Fondamental Model of Resonance.

Return type:

List

resonantstate.SFMR.topology(delta)[source]

Returns \([Xmin, Xmax, Xres, Xint, Xhyp]\) where \(Xmin, Xmax\) are the position of the separatrix, the \(Xres\) the position of the primary fixed point, \(Xint\) the position of the secondary fixed point, and \(Xhyp\) the position of the hyperbolic fixed point, all in the \(sin r=0\) plane. If \(\delta < 0\), returns \([0, 0, Xres, 0, 0]\) When \(\delta >= 0\); then \(Xhyp <= Xint <= Xmin <= Xres <= Xmax\)

resonantstate.SFMR.get_SFMR_quantities(samples, pair, p=None, sampling=1)[source]

call functions of the package to extract the quantities used in the first paper : delta, DMMR, er, Der, es, RS, RT, p

Parameters:

  • samples (pandas dataframe) – posterior of a dynamical analysis

  • pair (tuple of ints) – identifiant of the pair to consider

  • p (int, optional) – p+1:p first order MMR to consider

  • sampling (int, optional) – thinning of the samples

Returns:

  • Dictionary with the following keys

  • delta (np.array of floats of the lengh of the thinned samples) – \(\delta\) parameter of the second fundamental model for resonance (Henrard and Lemaitre 1983)

  • DMMR (np.array of floats of the lengh of the thinned samples) – \(\\xi_r^2-3(\delta+1)\)

  • er (np.array of floats of the lengh of the thinned samples) – \(\\xi_r\) parameter of the second fundamental model for resonance (see GRSW paper 1 - Leleu et al 2026) also called resonant eccentricities

  • Der (np.array of floats of the lengh of the thinned samples) – \(\Delta \\xi_r\) parameter of the second fundamental model for resonance (see GRSW paper 1 - Leleu et al 2026)

  • es (np.array of floats of the lengh of the thinned samples) – \(\\xi_s\) secular eccentricities (see GRSW paper 1 - Leleu et al 2026)

  • RS (np.array of floats of the lengh of the thinned samples) –

    position of the sample in the second fundamental model:

    2 : upper libration 1 : formally resonant 0 : upper circulation -1 : lower circulation -2 : lower libration

  • p (int) – pair attributed to the p+1:p MMR
